\section{Conclusion}
The Traffic Timing Assistant specifies how to connect a three-plan traffic decision to an observable FPGA phase controller. In two proposal-reported synthetic comparisons, aggregate queue vehicle-seconds fell relative to fixed balanced timing, while some paired scenarios worsened. A separate development-board summary reports plan application. The next evaluation requires matched traffic traces, a rule-only comparator and time-aligned board state. These artifacts would let investigators distinguish the effect of plan choice from delay in execution, quantify regressions by direction and verify the timing of plan handoff. Unified task intake and reporting are the remaining integration priorities.
